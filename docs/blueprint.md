# **App Name**: AuditFlow

## Core Features:

- Finding Logging: Log and categorize audit findings with title, details, and risk level.
- Mitigation Planning: Establish mitigation strategies for identified risks, agreed upon with the branch.
- Progress Tracking: Track the progress of agreed-upon mitigation to see if the identified gap is fixed.
- Automated Reminders: Automated email reminders for scheduled re-validations and case closures.
- AI Mitigation Tool: AI-powered tool for suggesting common risk mitigation strategies based on the type of finding and risk level.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep Blue (#3F51B5) to convey trust, security, and professionalism, reflecting the seriousness of audits.
- Background color: Light Gray (#F0F0F5), to provide a neutral backdrop that doesn't distract from the content.
- Accent color: Light Purple (#BA68C8), as it's analogous to blue, but creates contrast.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font known for its clarity and readability on screens.
- Use simple, clear, and consistent icons throughout the application to aid in navigation and quickly identify functions. Ensure icons are high contrast for visibility.
- Prioritize a clean and organized layout. Information should be easy to find and navigate. Use card-based layouts for audit findings, making it easy to scan and digest information.
- Subtle transitions and animations can be used to provide feedback to user actions, such as confirming an action or loading new data. Avoid excessive animations that could distract or slow down the user experience.
